# Asset License

The `assets/` directory is intentionally shipped as an empty folder structure.

No artwork, sprites, textures, sound effects, audio, or music are provided in this
template. You are expected to populate the `assets/` folders with content you are
legally allowed to use (your own work, properly licensed assets, or freely licensed
CC0/CC-BY assets).

**IMPORTANT:** Never commit or distribute assets you do not have the rights to use.
Always record the source and license of every third-party asset you add, for example
in this file or a per-asset `LICENSE` note.
